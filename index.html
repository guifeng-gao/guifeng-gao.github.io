About us

高

Dr. Gui-Feng Gao (高贵锋)

Associate Research Fellow
Dr. Gui-Feng Gao received his Ph.D. in Ecology from Xiamen University in 2018. He completed his postdoctoral training at the Institute of Soil Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences. His research focuses on soil microbiome ecology, microbial community assembly, and ecosystem functioning.

Research Directions

  2. Soil Microbial Community Structure and Functional Evolution - Linking microbial composition to ecosystem functions and services
