About us

高

Dr. Gui-Feng Gao (高贵锋)

Associate Research Fellow
Dr. Gui-Feng Gao received his Ph.D. in Ecology from Xiamen University in 2018. He completed his postdoctoral training at the Nanjing Institute of Soil Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2019-2021), where he served as Assistant Research Fellow (2021-2023) and is currently serving as Associate Research Fellow (since 2023). His research focuses on soil microbiome biogeography and the assembly processes of microbial communities in coastal wetland ecosystems under environmental change.
